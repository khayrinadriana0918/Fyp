  1. What is the Student Issue Management System?

    2. The Student Issue Management System is a web-based platform that allows students to submit academic or administrative issues to their Head of Programme (HoP), track the progress of their requests, and receive feedback or updates through one centralized system.

  2. Who Can Use This System?

    3. The system is intended for students from the course CC101, CT204, CT206, and the Heads of Programme (HoP) from the same courses, and system administrators. Students use it to submit and track requests, while HoPs manage and respond to requests from students in their programme.

  3. What type of issues can I submit?

    4. You can submit academic or administrative issues related to your programme, such as course registration matters, timetable conflicts, section changes, inactive status, or other programme-related requests. You can see category issues on the request form when submitting a form.

  4. How do I submit a new request?

    5. Check out the User Manual to see the guides on how to submit a new request depending on your role in the system.

  5. Can I upload document to my request?

    6. Yes. You can upload supporting document if they are required for your request.

  6. Who will review my request?

    7. As a student, the request will be reviewed and managed by the Head of Programme responsible for your academic programme. As a head of programme, the request will be reviewed and managed by the system admins for this system.

  7. Is my request information stored in the system?

    8. Yes. Submitted requests are stored in the system database so that they can be managed, monitored, and retrieved throughout the resolution process.
